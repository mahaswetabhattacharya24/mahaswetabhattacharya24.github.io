Mahasweta Bhattacharya

Senior Scientist · Sanofi · Cambridge, MA

I build AI systems for biological discovery — multimodal foundation models, agentic LLM pipelines, and graph neural networks that turn heterogeneous biomedical data into actionable therapeutic hypotheses.

Before Sanofi I was a postdoctoral fellow at Johns Hopkins (Radiation Oncology), and I completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering at the University at Buffalo, working on neural signal processing, functional connectomics, and brain–computer interfaces.

Research

Multimodal foundation models for therapeutic discovery. Unified representations across human genetics, bulk & single-cell transcriptomics, and clinical-trial outcomes; achieved a 4× improvement in causal target recall over genetics-only baselines and a transferable backbone for cross-disease generalization.

Agentic LLM pipelines for scientific evidence synthesis. Production multi-agent systems orchestrating Claude-based agents to autonomously aggregate genetics, transcriptomics, and clinical literature into structured target reports. Reduced expert manual review by >50% across 30+ therapeutic programs.

Graph neural networks for combinatorial drug design. GNN platform integrating protein–protein interaction graphs, synergy metrics, and LLM-guided evidence retrieval; generated 5 novel bispecific target-pair candidates for immunology indications.

Deep reinforcement learning & GPU-accelerated physics. RL agent for VMAT machine-parameter optimization (deliverable plans in 3.3s, statistically significant rectum-dose reduction, p=0.024); GPU-accelerated proton pencil-beam dose engine (0.28s vs 4.68s Monte Carlo, 96% 3D-gamma agreement).

Computational neuroscience. Functional connectome inference from longitudinal two-photon calcium imaging; multimodal NIRS–EEG biomarkers of consciousness; computational modeling of transcranial photobiomodulation.

GPU-Accelerated Proton Dose Engine

Python/CUDA double-Gaussian proton pencil-beam dose engine. 0.28s mean patient-plan vs. 4.68s Monte Carlo; 96% 3D-gamma agreement (2%/2mm). Open-source Python package; JACMP 2025.

[Johns Hopkins · 2023] · GPU · CUDA · open source

Deep RL for VMAT Optimization

DRL agent producing deliverable prostate VMAT plans in 3.3s; reduced mean rectum dose vs. clinical plans (17.4 vs. 21.0 Gy, p=0.024) on 15-patient external cohort. Medical Physics 2024.

[Johns Hopkins · 2023] · reinforcement learning · radiotherapy

FARCI — Fast and Robust Connectome Inference

MATLAB toolbox for fast functional-connectome inference from calcium imaging using OASIS spike deconvolution and partial correlations. Outperformed state-of-the-art on NCC and NAOMi benchmarks.

[SUNY Buffalo · 2021] · neuroscience · open source
