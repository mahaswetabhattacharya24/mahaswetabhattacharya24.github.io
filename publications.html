A Python Package for Fast GPU-Based Proton Pencil Beam Dose Calculation
Mahasweta Bhattacharya, Calin Reamy, Heng Li, Junghoon Lee, William T. Hrinivich
Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics, 2025
journal
Open-source Python/CUDA package for double-Gaussian proton pencil-beam dose calculation. Achieves mean per-patient runtime of 0.28s vs. 4.68s for Monte Carlo, with 96% 3D-gamma agreement at the 2%/2mm criterion across a clinical proton-therapy validation cohort.

Clinical VMAT Machine Parameter Optimization for Localized Prostate Cancer Using Deep Reinforcement Learning
William T. Hrinivich, Mahasweta Bhattacharya, Lina Mekki, Todd McNutt, Xun Jia, Heng Li, Daniel Y. Song, Junghoon Lee
Medical Physics 51(6), 3972–3984, 2024
journal
Deep reinforcement learning agent for direct optimization of VMAT machine parameters for prostate cancer. Produces deliverable plans in 3.3s with statistically significant rectum-dose reduction vs. clinical plans (17.4 vs. 21.0 Gy mean, p = 0.024) on a 15-patient external cohort.

Computational Modeling of the Photon Transport, Tissue Heating, and Cytochrome C Oxidase Absorption during Transcranial Near-Infrared Stimulation
Mahasweta Bhattacharya, Anirban Dutta
Brain Sciences 9(8), 179, 2019
journal
Multilayer Monte Carlo + bioheat finite-element simulation of transcranial near-infrared stimulation in human head models. Quantifies photon-fluence, tissue temperature rise, and cytochrome-c-oxidase absorption — providing a biophysical basis for safe parameter selection in photobiomodulation.
