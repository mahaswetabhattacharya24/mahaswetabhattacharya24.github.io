About Me

Researcher. Engineer. Scientist.

I am a Senior Scientist at Sanofi in Cambridge, MA, with a track record of building AI systems that directly advance drug discovery — 7 novel targets into preclinical evaluation, 3 preclinical nominations across 30+ therapeutic programs, and a 4× improvement in causal target recall over genetics-only baselines. I design agentic LLM pipelines, multimodal foundation models, and graph-based discovery platforms that turn biological complexity into actionable decisions.

Before Sanofi, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Radiation Oncology at Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, where I developed GPU-accelerated proton dose engines and deep reinforcement learning frameworks for radiotherapy optimization.

I completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering at the University at Buffalo, focusing on neural signal processing, brain-computer interfaces, functional connectomics, and computational photobiomodulation modeling. I hold an MS in Electrical Engineering and a BTech in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

Outside of research, I mentor students through MIT UPOP and the Hopkins Biotech Network, and actively contribute as a peer reviewer for multiple leading journals.

Selected Work

Research Projects

Agentic AI · Drug Discovery
Agentic Target Credentialing Pipeline
Orchestrated Claude-based multi-agent system that autonomously aggregates multimodal biological evidence (genetics, transcriptomics, literature) and generates structured plain-language target reports. Reduced manual review overhead by >50% in a regulated scientific workflow.
Foundation Model · Multimodal ML
Multimodal Target Discovery Foundation Model
Integrated genetics, transcriptomics, and clinical embeddings into a unified representation space. Achieved 4× improvement in causal target recall over genetics-only baselines. Establishes a transferable backbone for cross-disease generalization.
Graph AI · Bispecific Discovery
Graph-Based Bispecific Target Discovery Platform
Graph neural network platform integrating synergy metrics, biological embeddings, and LLM-guided evidence retrieval to identify bispecific antibody opportunities. Generated 5 novel bispecific target-pair candidates for immunology indications.
Indication Discovery · Scaling
Scalable Indication Discovery Engine
Computational repurposing engine scoring 232 immune indications in 3 weeks and scaling to 17,000+ phenotypes. Enables whitespace identification and automated indication prioritization across the immunology portfolio.
Radiation Oncology · GPU Computing
GPU-Accelerated Proton Dose Engine
Python package implementing a double-Gaussian proton pencil beam dose algorithm on GPU. Achieves 0.28s mean computation vs 4.68s Monte Carlo with 96% 3D-gamma agreement — enabling ML-scale throughput for clinical treatment planning.
Deep RL · Radiotherapy
RL-Based VMAT Optimization
Deep reinforcement learning framework for automated VMAT machine-parameter optimization. Produces deliverable prostate plans in 3.3s and significantly reduces mean rectum dose (17.4 vs 21.0 Gy, p=0.024) versus clinical plans on a 15-patient external cohort.
Neuroscience · Connectomics
FARCI: Connectome Inference Toolbox
MATLAB toolbox for fast and robust connectome inference from calcium imaging data using OASIS spike deconvolution and partial correlations. Outperforms existing spike-inference algorithms on NCC and NAOMi benchmarks in both accuracy and scalability.
Neuroimaging · Brain Injury
Consciousness Biomarker via NIRS–EEG
Multimodal neuroimaging approach combining portable fNIRS and EEG to identify frontal HbO–EEG coupling signatures (0.07–0.13 Hz) as consciousness biomarkers. AMICA + k-NN achieved >90% accuracy distinguishing conscious vs unresponsive patients.
